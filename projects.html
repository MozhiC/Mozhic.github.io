Selected work

Projects

My projects explore how modern AI can be combined with the physical structure, temporal dynamics, and operational requirements of power and energy systems.

01

LLMs, Agents & Foundation Models for Power Systems

Developing large-language-model-based approaches for power-system analysis and spatiotemporal learning. Current work combines grid-aware representations with pretrained models for tasks such as contingency detection, transmission-line failure localization, energy time-series modeling, and data-efficient learning, while also exploring agent-oriented workflows for future grid analysis and operation.

Large Language Models Agents Spatiotemporal Learning Contingency Analysis
02

High-Order Topological Learning for Transmission Systems

Studying higher-order grid structure through electrical distance, persistent homology, and topology-aware graph learning. The work builds representations that capture interactions beyond immediate neighbors and combines them with graph and temporal models for robust transmission-contingency identification, including N-1 and sequential N-1-1 events.

Persistent Homology Electrical Distance Higher-Order Learning Transmission Resilience
03

Graph Representation Learning for Power Grids

Designing graph-based representations that preserve electrical connectivity and spatial dependencies in grid data. Applications include transmission-network partitioning, outage detection and localization, spatiotemporal grid modeling, and other learning tasks where the network topology is as important as the measurements themselves.

Graph Neural Networks Grid Topology Network Partitioning Representation Learning
04

Plug Power Fuel Cell Stack Break-In Process Forecasting

Building an AI-assisted forecasting framework for the break-in stage of commercial hydrogen fuel-cell stacks using real manufacturing data from Plug Power. The approach uses feature selection and attention-enhanced ConvLSTM forecasting to predict future stack-voltage behavior, then combines the forecast with domain knowledge to support earlier break-in completion decisions and factory acceptance.

Hydrogen Fuel Cells ConvLSTM Attention Manufacturing AI
Industry collaboration: Plug Power
